A pilot interface system for intelligent mobility — translating vehicle perception, environmental context, and automated driving intent into a clear, calm, and trustworthy cockpit experience.

Overview

The overall design demo presents the core features of the Pilot HMI: context-aware information hierarchy, readable driving intent, scenario-based camera perspectives, and a calm visual language that helps users understand what the vehicle sees, predicts, and plans to do next.

Design Principle

From system perception
to human confidence.

Environment Design

Adaptive atmosphere
across the day.

Morning · Day · Dusk · Night

The environment system adapts the Pilot HMI across four moments: early morning, daytime, dusk, and night. Color temperature, contrast, lighting emphasis, and visual density shift with ambient conditions, ensuring the cockpit remains legible, comfortable, and emotionally aligned with the journey.

Feature System

Other features that make
Pilot simple.

01

Context Center

A central information hub that gathers the most relevant driving context at each moment: current automation state, system confidence, route intent, and upcoming maneuvers. Instead of scattering signals across the interface, Context Center creates one readable focal point.

02

Serialized Obstacle Models

Obstacles are represented as a consistent visual system rather than isolated object icons. Vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists, cones, and temporary road elements share a serialized modeling logic, making perception easier to scan and compare at a glance.

03

Scenario-Based Perspectives

The camera perspective changes according to the driving scenario. Highway cruising, lane change, intersection approach, tight road, and parking-adjacent moments each receive a view that best communicates spatial relationships and vehicle intent.

04

Silent Mapping

Mapping feedback is present but quiet. The system continuously builds and updates environmental understanding in the background, surfacing only the signals that help the user feel oriented and confident without turning perception into visual noise.
